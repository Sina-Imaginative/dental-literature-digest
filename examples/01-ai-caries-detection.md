# Example Output 1 — AI in Dental Caries Detection

## Abstract Source
PubMed — Reliability of AI software for detecting approximal caries lesions on bitewing radiographs.

## Structured Breakdown

**KEY FINDING**
The AI software (Nostic) achieved an accuracy of 0.85 for detecting dentinal caries lesions and 0.78 for enamel lesions on bitewing radiographs, performing decently compared to a five-dentist consensus gold standard.

**CLINICAL RELEVANCE**
This tool could serve as a reliable second-opinion aid in daily practice, particularly for catching early proximal caries that are easy to miss visually. Dentists could use it to reduce diagnostic errors on bitewing radiographs without replacing their own judgment.

**LIMITATIONS**
The sample size was small — only 40 radiographs and 576 surfaces — which limits generalizability. The study also tested only one commercial AI software, so findings may not apply to other AI diagnostic tools.

**ONE-LINER TAKEAWAY**
AI detects dentinal caries better than enamel caries on bitewings — promising as a support tool, not a replacement.
