# Example Output 2 — Periodontal Disease and Systemic Health

## Abstract Source
PubMed — Narrative review on the links between periodontal diseases and systemic health conditions.

## Structured Breakdown

**KEY FINDING**
Periodontal pathogens like Porphyromonas gingivalis drive systemic diseases — including cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and Alzheimer's — through persistent inflammation, endotoxemia, and immune modulation, with emerging multiplex biomarker platforms showing strong potential for early detection.

**CLINICAL RELEVANCE**
Dentists are not just treating gums — they are at the frontline of detecting systemic disease risk. This review supports the case for integrating point-of-care salivary biomarker testing into routine periodontal appointments as a tool for early systemic disease screening.

**LIMITATIONS**
As a narrative review, it lacks the methodological rigor of a systematic review or meta-analysis, meaning study selection was not standardized and conclusions may reflect author bias. Most cited diagnostic technologies remain experimental and are not yet validated for widespread clinical use.

**ONE-LINER TAKEAWAY**
Sick gums, sick body — periodontal pathogens are systemic disease drivers, and the mouth is an underused diagnostic window.
