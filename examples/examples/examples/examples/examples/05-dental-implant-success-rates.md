# Example Output 5 — Dental Implant Success Rates

## Abstract Source
PubMed — Systematic review and meta-analysis on implant survival and success in patients with autoimmune diseases.

## Structured Breakdown

**KEY FINDING**
A meta-analysis of 14 studies found that dental implants show favorable survival rates in autoimmune patients overall, but rheumatoid arthritis patients showed significantly higher marginal bone loss of 1.60mm compared to healthy controls.

**CLINICAL RELEVANCE**
Clinicians should not categorically exclude autoimmune patients from implant treatment, but rheumatoid arthritis and diabetes require tailored treatment planning and closer follow-up monitoring for marginal bone loss. A one-size-fits-all implant protocol is insufficient for this population.

**LIMITATIONS**
All 14 included studies were observational, meaning causality cannot be established and confounding factors like medication use were difficult to control. The wide survival rate range of 76.4% to 100% across studies reflects significant variability in patient populations and implant protocols.

**ONE-LINER TAKEAWAY**
Implants work in autoimmune patients — but rheumatoid arthritis is the red flag that demands extra monitoring for bone loss.
