# Example Output 4 — Oral Cancer Early Detection

## Abstract Source
PubMed — Systematic review on the application of artificial intelligence in early diagnosis and risk prediction of oral cancer.

## Structured Breakdown

**KEY FINDING**
A systematic review of 63 studies found that AI models demonstrate high sensitivity and specificity in detecting early oral lesions and differentiating precancerous tissue, with a clear trend toward multimodal and lightweight high-performance models.

**CLINICAL RELEVANCE**
AI-powered screening tools could dramatically reduce late-stage oral cancer diagnoses by offering a non-invasive, operator-independent alternative to traditional screening — particularly valuable in low-resource settings where specialist access is limited.

**LIMITATIONS**
Most studies were limited by small sample sizes, lack of external validation, and poor model interpretability, meaning clinicians cannot yet fully trust or explain AI decisions in a real patient encounter. Ethical and data privacy concerns also remain unresolved.

**ONE-LINER TAKEAWAY**
AI can spot oral cancer early with high accuracy — but needs larger datasets, external validation, and transparency before it belongs in the clinic.
