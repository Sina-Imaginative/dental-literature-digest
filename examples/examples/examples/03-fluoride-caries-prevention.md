# Example Output 3 — Fluoride and Caries Prevention

## Abstract Source
PubMed — Systematic review and meta-analysis on the effectiveness of fluoride varnish in preventing dental caries.

## Structured Breakdown

**KEY FINDING**
A meta-analysis of 13 RCTs found that fluoride varnish significantly reduced caries across 4,784 participants, with a 54% reduction seen in high-risk groups receiving quarterly applications.

**CLINICAL RELEVANCE**
Fluoride varnish applied quarterly is most effective in young children and patients with xerostomia — two groups dentists should be proactively targeting for preventive protocols. This evidence directly supports integrating fluoride varnish into public health and high-risk patient programs.

**LIMITATIONS**
High heterogeneity across studies (I² = 75.1%) means results varied significantly between trials, reducing confidence in the pooled estimate. The lack of standardized application protocols across studies makes direct clinical translation difficult.

**ONE-LINER TAKEAWAY**
Fluoride varnish works — especially quarterly in kids and dry-mouth patients — but standardized protocols are needed to maximize its impact.
